# Privacy policy

OpenCal AI has no account and no server. The developers receive nothing from the app.

## Stored on your phone

Meals, photos, weight, measurements, water, exercise, recipes, settings, and corrections of recognition results. They stay in the app's
private folder until you export or delete them. Photos are saved without location and camera data. System cloud backup is switched off.

## What can leave the phone, and when

| To | What | When |
|---|---|---|
| Open Food Facts (world.openfoodfacts.org) | the barcode, or the words you search | you scan a barcode, or tap "Search packaged foods online". Can be switched off |
| Hugging Face (huggingface.co) | an ordinary file request | you download a model |
| A server you enter | meal photos (small, no metadata), your notes, or an assistant question with your averages | only after you switch it on |
| Your phone's speech service | your voice | you tap Speak. On Android 12 and newer it is done on the phone if a language pack is installed; otherwise the provider of the service, often Google, may receive it |
| Health Connect (on the phone) | what you allow | you switch it on |

These services see your IP address like any website. Their own privacy rules apply.

## Permissions

Camera (scanning), microphone (Speak), notifications (reminders and downloads), Health Connect. All optional, asked when needed.

## Your choices

Export everything (Profile, Your data), delete everything (same place), lock the app, or uninstall it, which erases all data.

The app is not for children under 13. It shows no calorie targets to people under 18.
